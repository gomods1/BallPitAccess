BALL x PIT Access

A mod that makes BALL x PIT (Kenny Sun / Devolver Digital) playable without sight. It speaks every menu through your screen reader, lets you use the whole game with the keyboard, and turns the battlefield into sound: enemies, your balls, enemy shots, XP crystals and special items all have their own sounds, coming from their side of you.

Version 0.5.0, for game version 1.308, including the Risen Ballbylon DLC. The mod speaks every language the game offers and follows the game’s language setting.

What you need

  • BALL x PIT on Windows (Steam), version 1.308.
  • A screen reader: NVDA is the one tested. Others supported by the Prism speech library (JAWS, Narrator, SAPI voices) should work too.
  • MelonLoader 0.7.3 (x64), the mod loader. Free, from its official GitHub releases page.
  • Headphones or stereo speakers: left and right matter a lot in battle.

Installing

With the installer (easiest): download BallPitAccessibilityInstaller.exe from the latest release and run it. It finds the game through Steam, installs MelonLoader 0.7.3, the Prism speech library and the mod, and later updates or removes them. Then start the game: the first start takes a few minutes and is silent while MelonLoader prepares the game’s files, then your screen reader starts speaking.

By hand: download BallPitAccess-vX.Y.Z.zip from the same page and extract everything into the game folder (by default C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\BALLxPIT, the folder that contains Balls.exe), so that version.dll ends up next to Balls.exe. The zip already contains MelonLoader: you do not install it separately. Start the game; the first start is slow and silent, as above.

Removing the mod

The installer’s Uninstall button removes the mod and MelonLoader and keeps your settings in UserData. By hand:

Delete Mods\BallPitAccess.dll and prism.dll. To remove MelonLoader too, delete version.dll and the folders MelonLoader, Mods, Plugins and UserData from the game folder. No game file is changed by the mod.

First steps

  • Press F1 at any time for the help: every part of it, starting with the part for where you are. Up and Down arrows read it line by line, Tab jumps to the next part, Escape closes.
  • Press F4 for the mod’s settings, and inside them open the sound guide: it plays every battle sound and says what it means. Listening to it once before your first run helps a lot.
  • The game itself is mouse driven with a keyboard; with the mod you never need the mouse except for the idle launcher in the base (see Known limits).

Menus

Every screen is one list in a sensible order: its choices first, then its buttons, then Close. The order never depends on where things are drawn on the screen.

Enter Choose
Tab / Shift+Tab Next / previous group of the screen
Home / End First / last item of the group
Left / Right Change a list or slider setting; on a level, change its difficulty
Ctrl+Tab / Ctrl+Shift+Tab Next / previous tab on screens with tabs
Backspace or Escape Back
Page Up / Page Down Set in the encyclopedia; new game plus tier in the level list

  • Enter on a character puts you on its Select button.
  • When the guided tutorial points at something, focus jumps to it: press Enter.
  • Upgrade choices are read with their numbers (“Deals 10 to 15 damage”).

Battle sounds

Four rules hold for every sound:

  • Side: a sound comes from the side of the thing it is about.
  • Pitch: higher means closer.
  • Muffled means it is behind you.
  • A sound that asks you to move comes from the side to go to.

Things that move all the time (your ball, enemy shots) are continuous sounds that follow them; things that change in steps (enemies advancing, items lying on the floor) are short sounds.

Sound Meaning
Hand drum An enemy. Higher and faster = closer. The three nearest play in turn.
Big deep drum A boss. Drums, gong and horn when one arrives.
Snarl An enemy is about to reach you.
Rising whine An enemy winds up an attack, on its side.
Rolling hum (continuous) The ball you can catch. Walk toward its side; it rises as you get closer to where the ball will land, throbs faster as the ball nears you, and is softer while the ball can still bounce off an enemy.
Click as the hum steps up You are under the ball: stay there.
Glove thud Ball caught.
Dull sinking thud A ball got past you.
Gun rack Your balls are back: you can shoot (silent with autofire).
Glass tink The nearest XP crystal, gold or resource you can walk to. They do not collect themselves.
Kalimba sparkle A special item: fuser, blueprint, egg, gear, health, magnet, bomb, gem bag.
Airy whistle (continuous) An enemy shot flying near you, higher as it comes. Louder when it will hit you.
Swoosh and crack A shot will hit you: step to the side it sounds on.
Electric hum / two zaps A laser is aiming / you are in its line: step to the side it sounds on.
Falling whistle / fuse hiss A bomb is falling nearby / it will land on you: step to the side it sounds on.
Squelch You stepped on a floor hazard (its name is spoken).
Punch / gulp Damage / healing.
Heartbeat Low health; faster when very low.
Woozy wobble A status effect landed on you.
Two bell notes up A level up is ready (press E).
Wooden knock Side edge of the arena (also the end of a menu list).
Metal knock Front or back limit of your walking area.
Switch click The aim assist changed target, on the side of the new one.

Every one of these can be heard in F4 → Sound guide, with Left / Right to hear it on each side.

Sound filters (number keys)

In battle, the number keys mute or unmute a group of sounds so you can concentrate. The game uses none of these keys.

Key Group
1 Enemies: drums, snarl, wind-up, boss arrival
2 Balls: rolling hum, click, catch, miss, gun rack
3 Rewards: crystals, gold, resources, special items
4 Shots and hazards
5 Arena edges
6 Your health: damage, healing, heartbeat, status
7 Aim assist click
0 Everything back on
  • Each number switches its own group, independently of the others.
  • Ctrl+number keeps only that group. Example: enemies and rewards only = Ctrl+1, then 3.
  • Your choice is kept for the next session. When a battle starts with something muted, the mod says what is muted.
  • Muting changes only the sounds: the H, J, P, N keys still answer and the catch assist still dodges.

Assists

Everything an assist does can also be done by ear; the assists are there when you want them.

  • Aim assist (F5 changes the mode): aims for you at the closest enemy (default), the strongest enemy, the enemy in front of you, a fixed angle, or off (mouse). The arrows fine-tune it.
  • Catch assist (hold Shift): the hero walks under the next ball coming down; with no ball coming it walks to the nearest crystal or item; if a shot is about to hit you it steps out of the way first. W / S still move you forward and back, A / D always take over. In F4 it can be off, “hold Shift” (default) or automatic.

Mod settings (F4)

Up / Down move, Left / Right or Enter change, Escape closes. Settings are saved.

  • Aim assist mode
  • Catch assist: off, hold Shift, automatic
  • Audio cues on / off, cue volume
  • Enemy radar, ball sounds, arena edges, shots and hazards (on / off)
  • Pickup sounds: off, special items only, all
  • Health sounds, aim assist click (on / off)
  • Read upgrade descriptions on focus
  • Sound guide (Enter opens)
  • Debug logging

Tips for a first run

  • Catch your balls. A caught ball can be shot again at once; a missed one takes 2 to 3 seconds to come back. Follow the rolling hum until you hear the click, then stay.
  • Pick up your XP crystals. They do not collect themselves. Follow the glass tink; muffled means it is behind you.
  • Survive first. Early in a run, staying alive matters more than damage.
  • Fuse and evolve your balls when the level up offers it: the choices say “fusion” or “evolution”.
  • Slow down for bosses with the minus key. The mod reminds you when a boss arrives at a high speed.
  • Dying is normal: the gold you bring back builds New Ballbylon, and buildings make every later run stronger.

Known limits

  • The idle launcher in the base still needs the mouse.
  • Leaderboards and Twitch voting are read only in a basic way. The credits cannot be skipped.
  • Mod settings are on F4, not inside the game’s own options screen.
  • The mod’s own texts in languages other than English and Spanish were translated without a review by a native speaker yet. Corrections are welcome. Names from the game (buildings, balls, enemies, upgrades) always come from the game’s own translation.
  • Sound volumes, rates and the catch width have not been tuned with players yet.

Reporting a problem

Useful files, in the game folder:

  • UserData\BallPitAccess\speech.log: everything the mod said.
  • MelonLoader\Latest.log: the mod’s log (errors, screens, catches and misses).
  • In battle, Ctrl+Shift+D writes UserData\BallPitAccess\threats.txt (enemies, balls, shots, pickups with positions) and the open screen’s list to Latest.log: useful when a sound points the wrong way or a menu reads in the wrong order.

Say what you did, what you heard, and what you expected.
